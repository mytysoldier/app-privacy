Support

For bug reports, questions, or feedback, please use the contact form. You may write your message in English.

The form labels are in Japanese. Use this guide to complete it:

  • お問い合わせ種別 (required): inquiry type. Choose 不具合報告 (bug report), 使い方の質問 (how-to question), 改善要望 (suggestion), or その他 (other).
  • お問い合わせ内容 (required): your message.
  • 連絡先メールアドレス (optional): contact email if you would like a reply.
  • 端末情報 (optional): device and iOS version.
  • 再現手順 (optional): steps to reproduce a problem.
  • 添付ファイル (optional): attachment, up to one file and 10 MB. Google may require sign-in for file uploads.

Select 送信 to submit. Please do not include journal text, recordings, authentication tokens, or API keys.
